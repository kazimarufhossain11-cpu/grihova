Why Grihova

Less guesswork between you and the right property.

What the platform does for you — and, just as clearly, what it doesn’t.

The same facts on every listing

Price, size in sqft, floor, facing, handover status and service charge sit in the same place, so comparing two flats takes seconds.

Inquiries go to the right person

Your message reaches the owner or the agent named on the listing — not a call centre.

Viewings on your schedule

Pick a day and time slot from the listing page. The lister confirms or suggests another time.

Know who you’re dealing with

Every listing shows whether it comes from an owner, an agent or a developer, with their profile and other listings.

Labels that say exactly what was checked

A check mark names the check and its date. No listing is labelled “verified” in general, and none is a guarantee of clean title.

Professional help when you need it

Lawyers, surveyors and valuers are available through partner referrals, clearly marked as independent services.

How it works

From first search to keys in hand

Start searching
  1. 01

    Search for a property

    Filter by area, type, budget and bedrooms. Save the ones worth a second look — they stay on your device until you sign in.

  2. 02

    Compare options and connect

    Put shortlisted listings side by side, read what each label means, and send a question straight to the lister.

  3. 03

    Arrange a viewing, then decide

    Book a slot, visit in person, and bring in a lawyer or surveyor through our partners before any booking money changes hands.
